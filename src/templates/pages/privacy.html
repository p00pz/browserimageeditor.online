The short version

Your images stay on your device because processing happens in this browser. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or advertising. The browser may remember your theme and whether you dismissed the optional install prompt, and it caches application files so visited tools can work offline. Those caches contain site resources, never your images.

Your images

Images you process are decoded and re-encoded inside your browser tab. They are not uploaded, not copied to a server, not scanned and not stored. This is not a promise about our behaviour that you have to take on trust: the shipped code contains no upload requests at all, so there is no code path that could send a file. You can verify it by opening your browser's Network panel and using any tool — no request is made while an image is processed.

What is stored on your device

The theme choice is stored in local storage as browserimageeditor:theme when you change it. Your saved tool favorites (tool names only) are stored as browserimageeditor:favorites. Dismissing the optional install prompt is stored as browserimageeditor:install-dismissed. None of these values contains an image or is sent to a server. To support offline use, the browser's Cache Storage keeps pages and application resources you have visited. That cache does not store selected images or processed results. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes these preferences and cached resources.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies, which is why you will never see a cookie banner here. There is no analytics script, no advertising script, no tracking pixel, no session recording, and no third-party script, font or asset of any kind loaded into these pages. Nothing about your visit is profiled, and there is no account to log into.

What the server necessarily sees

The files that make up this site are served by a static hosting provider. Like every web server, it necessarily sees the request itself: your IP address, the page or file you asked for, your browser's user-agent string and the time of the request. That is standard operational logging, it happens on their infrastructure rather than in these pages, and it is not linked to any profile of you — there is no account or identifier to link it to. We do not run analytics over it and we do not sell or share it.

If you email us

Writing to {{contactEmail}} means we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, including any attachment you add. We use it to reply to you and nothing else: no newsletter, no marketing list, no sharing. If you would like that correspondence deleted afterwards, say so in the message or in a later one, and we will delete it.

Your rights over your data

We do not keep images or browser preferences on our servers. The theme, favorite tool names and install-prompt choices, plus cached application resources, stay in your browser and can be removed by clearing this site's data. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, ask and we will do it.

Children

The tools are not directed at children and collect nothing from anyone, so there is no separate children's data practice to describe.

Changes to this policy

If this page changes in a way that affects what happens to your data, the date below changes with it. Because the approach is "process locally, collect nothing", the honest expectation is that there will be very little to report.
